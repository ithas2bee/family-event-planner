Delete your Huddle Up account

You can request account deletion here without signing in or installing the app. Enter the email address on your account. We will send a confirmation link if it matches an account. If you do not receive one, try again later.

Your account is not deleted until you open the email link and confirm. The link expires after the time stated in the email and can be used once.

Request deletion

What happens to your information

  • Your account, identifying profile and authentication details, memberships, password-reset tokens, and account-linked private records are deleted.
  • Shared communities, events, polls, announcements, kickbacks, assignments, photos, and shared files are retained. Your creator, uploader, or member attribution is removed where supported. An assignment left without an assignee is marked as needed.
  • Shared text and captions are not scanned for personal information. Information included in shared content may remain visible to the community.
  • Photo files, backups, externally hosted profile images, provider logs, and other copies outside the API database are not deleted by this workflow. A minimal, non-identifying deletion audit record is retained for up to 30 days.

An account that is the only administrator of a community cannot be deleted until another administrator is assigned. If a verified request cannot be completed, contact Huddle Up support.

Confirm account deletion

Confirm only if you asked to delete your Huddle Up account. This action is permanent and cannot be undone.
